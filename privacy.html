Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

1. Overview

JP_Senior_Uploader ("the Application") is a personal automation tool operated by Minkuk Choi. The Application uses the YouTube API Services to upload original videos and Shorts created by the operator to the operator's own YouTube channel (こころの縁側) and to manage that channel's video metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, language, privacy status).

2. Data accessed

The Application accesses data only for the operator's own YouTube channel using OAuth 2.0 scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl. Data accessed includes: uploading the operator's own videos, reading and updating the operator's own video metadata, and setting thumbnails for the operator's own videos. The Application does not access, collect, or process any personal data of viewers, subscribers, or other users.

3. Data storage and retention

OAuth credentials (refresh token) are stored locally on the operator's private computer and are used solely to authenticate requests for the operator's own channel. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. Videos and metadata are owned and controlled by the operator via YouTube Studio and the Application.

4. Deletion and revocation

To delete videos uploaded through the Application, the operator deletes them in YouTube Studio. To revoke the Application's access entirely, the operator can remove "JP_Senior_Uploader" at any time at Google Account permissions; revocation is effective immediately. Local OAuth token files can be deleted by the operator at any time.

5. Google Privacy Policy

The Application complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Users of YouTube should review Google's privacy policy for how Google handles their data.
